# Group 10 Final Project

## Project Summary
When we began searching for a topic for our project to focus on, we had five candidates that we thought would be interesting to read further into. Those were:

1. Weather Prediction
2. Retirement Age Prediction
3. Mental Health Help Recommendation
4. Online Community Network Analysis
5. Tourism Recommendation

While we were interested in each topic, we decided to go with the third choice, a Recommendation System designed specifically for Mental Health Help. We believe that this topic has the most important outcome, because when completed, it has a great potential to serious help people's mental health.

Our research question is "How can a recommendation system suggest personalized mental wellness resources based on a user's self-reported emotions and experiences?" When we came up with this question, we decided that our approach for our machine modeling would be based on matching wellness resources to users based on their emotional state, experiences, and preferences, which we would determine using content based filtering. For example, a participant reports anxiety and academic stress, which leads to the system recommending certain breathing exercises, and an academic advisor program. 

## What we already know about our topic
We already know that nearly 1 in 7 people (mostly more) struggle with mental health issues.

We know that people are hesitant to reach out with their personal problems, for fear of alienation. This is why we want to provide an anonymous resource for them to help themselves.

We know that there are many different kinds of mental health issues, and that it's practically impossible to classify everyone underneath one umbrella. Everyone should have access to a personalized experience that caters to them, and is designed to best help them. 

## What we think we know about our topic, but must verify
We think we know which questions might apply best to users in order to cater to their specific individual situation. 

We think we know that people may be more comfortable exploring mental wellness resources anonymously, rather than seeking help directly from another person.

We think we know that personalized mental wellness resources are more effective than general recommendations. 

## What we need to learn about our topic
We need to learn the specifics around mental health issues so that we can properly assess the user without generalizing their problems. 

We need to learn how to best analyse the people who decide to reach out in order to give them the help that they need. 

We need to learn what exercises, advisor programs, and other forms of help best apply to each niche in each mental health issue.
